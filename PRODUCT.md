# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- The owner, who reviews every change to the repo on the live site after a push.
- Other Hermes Agent users who browse the catalog to find a skill, learn what it does, and open its SKILL.md or README to install it.
- Readers of the reports: long-form research write-ups (model and tool comparisons, benchmarks, integration guides) read start to finish.

## Product Purpose

my-hermes-skills is a public collection of Hermes Agent skills, plugins and bots, plus a library of dated research reports. The site at laansdole.github.io/my-hermes-skills is its catalog. It succeeds when a visitor can scan what exists, understand one skill from its card, and reach its source in one click, and when a report reads comfortably from top to bottom.

## Positioning

A working personal toolkit published as is: every skill is one someone uses day to day (systematic-review screening, Slack watchers, coding-agent orchestration, demo runbooks), and the reports are the research behind those choices.

## Operating Context

- The site is build output. `scripts/generate_docs.py` regenerates every page from `skills/**/SKILL.md` frontmatter and `reports/YYYY-MM/*.md` on each push via the GitHub Pages workflow. Pages are never hand-edited.
- Pages: skills catalog (`index.html`), reports catalog (`reports.html`), a compatibility redirect (`daily-news.html`), and one reader page per report (`reports/YYYY-MM/<slug>.html`).
- Skills are grouped into sections by top-level folder (Research, Productivity, Coding Agents, Design, Slack); nested skills render as children of their parent.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Light and dark themes with a toggle that follows the OS until the visitor overrides it, persisted in localStorage.
- Paragraphs and lists run the full column width. The owner rejects a narrow 65-75ch reading measure.
- Generated by a single Python script with no framework or front-end build step today.
- `scripts/verify_docs.py` (skills-repo-docs skill) and `tests/test_generate_docs_reports.py` check the output.

## Product Principles

- Scanning comes first in the catalog; reading comes first in a report.
- Generated, never hand-edited: the design lives in the generator.
